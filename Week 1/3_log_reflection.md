 2. Reflection: What the Data Can and Cannot Tell Me

What the data can tell me:
- How the prices of four items changed over twelve recorded dates.
- How prices differed between City 1 and City 2.
- Which items experienced the greatest price fluctuations.
- Which items had the highest average recorded prices.
- Whether prices increased or decreased during the recorded period.

What the data cannot tell me:
- The actual inflation rate in Pakistan because no CPI or inflation figures are provided.
- Why prices increased because production costs, supply and demand information are missing.
- Whether items became less affordable because household income and expenditure data are unavailable.
- The overall cost of living in Pakistan because the dataset only covers four items and two cities.

3. Unsupported AI Suggestions
- Investigating actual national inflation was unsupported because the dataset contains no inflation figures.
- Explaining the reasons behind price increases was unsupported because no information about production costs, supply or demand is available.
- Measuring affordability was unsupported because household income and expenditure data are not included.
